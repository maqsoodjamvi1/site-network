When I first started researching side hustles as an ITIN holder living in the US, I quickly realized how much conflicting information is out there. Many popular platforms instantly reject applications if you do not have a Social Security Number, leaving people feeling stuck. 

If you file taxes using an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, you have real earning potential, but you need to know which platforms actually process W-9 forms and 1099s using an ITIN. Over the past few years, I tested several remote gigs to see which ones successfully onboard ITIN holders without hitting a verification wall. Here are four verified remote options you can look into today.

## 1. Remote Freelance Writing and Content Creation

Freelance writing is one of the most accessible paths because clients rarely care about your legal status as long as you deliver good work and submit a valid tax form. Platforms like Upwork and Fiverr permit ITIN holders to register for accounts, verify their identity, and get paid. 

When you set up your profile on Upwork, you will eventually reach a tax verification step. Instead of an SSN, you can input your ITIN. The platform uses this to generate your annual 1099 form if you cross their earnings threshold. 

To get started this week, set up a profile focusing on a specific niche you know well—such as local business marketing, pet care, or SaaS software. Pitch small businesses directly via LinkedIn or local classifieds to bypass platform fees entirely. Direct clients usually pay via PayPal or Wise, which only require standard bank routing details and your tax ID for business accounts.

## 2. Online Transcription and Captioning

If you are a fast typist with a good ear for detail, transcription work offers flexible remote hours. Companies like Rev and GoTranscript hire independent contractors to turn audio and video files into text. 

During the onboarding phase for these platforms, you will be asked to fill out a W-9 form for US tax purposes. An ITIN is fully accepted on a W-9 form because the IRS issues them specifically for tax processing and reporting for individuals who are not eligible for an SSN. 

The application process usually involves a grammar quiz and a short transcription test. Once accepted, you can log in whenever you want, pick up audio files from a queue, and cash out your earnings weekly via PayPal. It is straightforward, requires zero upfront investment, and relies entirely on your typing speed and accuracy.

## 3. Remote Bilingual Customer Support and Moderation

Many US-based companies outsource their customer service or community management to remote independent contractors. Because the US is a multilingual country, companies frequently look for bilingual agents who can assist customers in Spanish, Mandarin, French, or Vietnamese.

Platforms like Omni Interactions and ModSquad contract out independent agents for chat support, email management, and community moderation for gaming or social media platforms. 

*   You apply directly through their contractor portals.
*   You choose your own hours from an available shift board.
*   You submit your ITIN during the contractor onboarding and background check phase.

These gigs often pay a flat hourly rate rather than per-task, making your weekly income much more predictable than freelance writing or transcription. 

## 4. Digital Product Sales on Independent Marketplaces

Instead of working for a platform that might have strict automated compliance filters, you can build your own digital storefront on marketplaces like Etsy or Gumroad. You sell digital assets—such as budgeting spreadsheets, social media templates, or sewing patterns—that customers download instantly.

Setting up an Etsy shop as an ITIN holder requires you to go through Etsy Payments. You will need to provide your legal name, home address, and your ITIN or EIN (Employer Identification Number) if you registered an LLC. Etsy uses this information to verify your identity and issue your 1099-K at the end of the year.

The main advantage here is ownership. No one is going to suddenly deactivate your account because of a mismatched background check database. You control the listings, the pricing, and the marketing. It takes a few weeks to get your first sale, but it creates a genuine independent business asset under your tax ID.

Navigating remote work with an ITIN requires patience, as automated corporate systems are often built with only SSN holders in mind. Sticking to platforms that explicitly accept W-9 forms with tax identification numbers will save you hours of frustration. Review your goals, pick one method that fits your daily schedule, and take your first step toward building stable remote income today.
