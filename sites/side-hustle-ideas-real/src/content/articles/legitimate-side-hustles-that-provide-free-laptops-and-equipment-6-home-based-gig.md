When I first started looking into home-based side hustles, my biggest roadblock wasn't finding the time—it was my aging laptop. It took ten minutes just to open a spreadsheet, and the battery died if I looked at it funny. Dropping a thousand dollars on new tech before I even made my first dollar felt like a huge financial risk. 

That is when I started hunting for side hustles that actually provide the equipment you need to do the job. 

Companies that invest in hardware are usually more established, meaning you are less likely to stumble into a scam or a fly-by-night operation. They send you a laptop, a headset, and sometimes even a second monitor because they need secure systems to protect their customer data. Here are six legitimate side hustles where providing a free laptop and equipment is standard practice.

## 1. Bilingual Customer Support Representative

Customer service is one of the most common fields that provides a fully equipped home office setup. If you are fluent in English and a second language—like Spanish, French, or Mandarin—your chances of getting hired and receiving a company laptop skyrocket. 

Companies like Apple, Omni Interactions, and various large telecom providers routinely ship out an iMac or PC laptop, a noise-canceling USB headset, and an ethernet cable. 

**What to expect:** You will go through a paid virtual training period. Before training starts, a heavy-duty box arrives on your doorstep containing everything except the desk and chair. The catch is that you usually must connect the computer directly to your router via an ethernet cable—Wi-Fi is rarely allowed due to security policies.

## 2. Search Engine Evaluator

If you prefer independent work over talking on the headset, search engine evaluation is worth looking into. Companies like Telus International and Appen hire contractors to rate the relevance of search engine results, map updates, and online advertisements. 

While some entry-level projects require your own device, higher-level evaluation contracts frequently require you to use a company-issued virtual machine or dedicated laptop to access proprietary rating guidelines.

**What to expect:** The work is flexible, usually requiring a minimum of 10 to 20 hours per week. You will pass an intensive qualification exam based on a 150-page guideline book before they ship you any hardware. It is detail-oriented work, but it pays decently and keeps you away from customer service queues.

## 3. Moderation and Trust & Safety Specialist

Social media platforms, gaming communities, and online marketplaces constantly need humans to review flagged content, handle user reports, and maintain platform safety. Because this work involves handling sensitive, graphic, or proprietary data, companies almost never let you use your personal computer.

Agencies that partner with major tech firms frequently hire remote moderators and ship encrypted laptops equipped with specialized VPNs and security software.

**What to expect:** This hustle can be emotionally heavy depending on the type of content you are reviewing. However, the pay rate is usually higher than standard data entry, and the technical setup is entirely managed by the company’s IT department. If a piece of hardware fails, they overnight a replacement.

## 4. Transcriptionist for Legal or Medical Services

General transcription doesn't always pay well, but legal and medical transcription require specialized knowledge and secure handling of confidential files. Medical clinics and legal firms will often provide a laptop along with industry software, foot pedals for audio control, and secure login credentials.

**What to expect:** You will likely need to prove your typing speed (usually 60+ words per minute) and pass an accuracy test. Once hired, the firm sends you the hardware kit so you can work securely within HIPAA or attorney-client privilege guidelines.

## 5. Corporate Chat Support Agent

Many retail brands and software-as-a-service (SaaS) companies have moved entirely away from phone support, favoring live chat and email tickets instead. This is ideal if you want a quieter work environment without phone calls.

Because these companies handle credit card numbers and personal data, they prefer issuing standardized, locked-down laptops rather than risking data leaks on personal machines.

**What to expect:** You will handle multiple chat conversations at once, requiring solid multi-tasking skills and fast typing. Companies hiring for these roles often provide the laptop, mouse, and keyboard, though you may need to supply your own high-speed internet connection.

## Tips for Landing an Equipment-Provided Gig

Before you apply, keep a few practical realities in mind to avoid wasting time on listings that turn out to be dead ends:

*   **Never pay for equipment:** Legitimate companies will never ask you to pay for your laptop, buy software upfront, or cash a check they send you to buy supplies yourself. If they ask for money, run.
*   **Check your internet speed:** Almost all of these gigs require a stable broadband connection. Run a speed test beforehand to ensure you meet their minimum upload and download requirements.
*   **Dedicate a quiet workspace:** Companies that send expensive equipment will often require a quiet, private room with a locking door during your shift, especially if you are handling customer data.

Finding a side hustle that lowers your startup costs by supplying the hardware is a great way to protect your personal finances while building a new income stream. Consider which style of work fits your daily routine best, and take a look at current openings from established remote employers to see which setup matches your needs.
