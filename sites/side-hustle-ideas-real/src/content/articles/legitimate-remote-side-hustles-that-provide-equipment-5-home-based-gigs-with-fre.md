Starting a side hustle sounds great until you realize you need to buy a $1,200 laptop, a noise-canceling headset, and an upgraded internet router just to get your foot in the door. Upfront costs can quickly kill a project before you earn your first dollar. 

When I first started hunting for extra income, I wanted something that didn't require raiding my savings account just to buy gear. That is when I started focusing specifically on remote gigs that ship the hardware directly to your doorstep. 

Working with company-provided equipment does more than save you money. It also keeps your personal computer free from corporate tracking software, and it ensures you have a machine powerful enough to run their specific internal apps without crashing. Here are five legitimate remote side hustles that routinely supply the hardware you need to get started.

## <h2>1. Virtual Customer Support and Chat Agent</h2>

This is the most common category for equipment-provided gigs. Companies handling sensitive financial, medical, or retail data cannot risk security breaches caused by outdated personal laptops, so they prefer to send you a locked-down workstation.

When I applied for a seasonal retail support role with a major sporting goods brand, a large box arrived three days before training started. Inside was a Dell laptop, an encrypted security key fob, a wired USB headset, and an external mouse. 

To land these roles, look for mid-to-large-size corporations rather than tiny agencies. Big box retailers, insurance companies, and utility providers usually have strict IT policies requiring company hardware. Your application needs to highlight your typing speed and problem-solving patience, but you won't need to mention your current computer setup because they are replacing it anyway.

## <h2>2. Remote Moderation and Trust-and-Safety Review</h2>

Platforms that host user-generated content—like video-sharing apps, online marketplaces, and gaming forums—frequently hire remote contractors to review flagged videos, images, and text. Because the work involves handling graphic or sensitive data, these companies use secure, monitored hardware.

These gigs usually involve reviewing a queue of reported posts and making fast judgment calls based on strict community guidelines. The hardware package typically includes a specialized desktop tower or a high-performance laptop, sometimes accompanied by dual monitors so you can cross-reference multiple policy documents at once. 

When searching for these roles on job boards, look for terms like "content analyst," "trust and safety specialist," or "community operations associate." Keep in mind that viewing flagged material can be mentally taxing, so make sure you are comfortable with that type of daily workflow before committing.

## <h2>3. Transcription and Closed Captioning for Media Outlets</h2>

While basic freelance transcription often requires you to use your own setup, working directly with broadcast networks, streaming services, or large localization agencies often comes with company gear. These companies need specialized software loaded onto your machine to handle proprietary media formats and secure video streams that cannot be downloaded to personal devices.

The equipment package usually features an encrypted laptop, specialized foot pedal controls for audio playback, and enterprise-grade over-ear headphones. 

To find these opportunities, bypass general freelance marketplaces and look at careers pages for media localization firms and captioning service providers. You will likely have to pass a timed grammar and typing test, but once you clear that hurdle, the onboarding team will send out your hardware kit.

## <h2>4. Technical Support and Help Desk Troubleshooting</h2>

If you have a knack for figuring out why printers won't connect or how to reset enterprise software permissions, tier-one technical support is a reliable way to secure a remote side hustle with free hardware. 

Tech companies and specialized IT staffing agencies prefer sending out standardized equipment because it makes troubleshooting your connection issues much easier for their internal help desk. If your company-issued laptop acts up, their IT team can remotely access it in seconds. 

Your welcome box for this type of gig often includes a VoIP desk phone or specialized headset adapter alongside the standard laptop and monitor. Emphasize your troubleshooting methodology and familiarity with ticketing systems like Zendesk or Jira when applying.

## <h2>5. Specialized Online English Teaching and Tutoring</h2>

While many online teaching platforms let you use any device that meets a minimum webcam spec, some international education corporations treat their independent contractors more like traditional remote staff. They supply branded laptops and high-definition webcams to maintain a consistent video and audio standard for students logging in from abroad.

This setup process usually involves a virtual hardware check where their IT department logs into your shipped device remotely to configure the teaching software and firewall settings before your first class. 

When filtering through tutoring listings, read the technical requirements section carefully. If a posting explicitly states "company laptop provided for lesson delivery," bookmark it immediately.

## <h2>What to Expect When the Equipment Arrives</h2>

Receiving company hardware comes with specific responsibilities. You will usually have to sign a property agreement making you financially responsible if the gear is lost or damaged through negligence. Always inspect the box for shipping damage immediately upon arrival and take photos of any dents.

Treat the equipment strictly as a professional workspace. Do not download personal games, let family members use the machine for browsing, or connect it to unsecured public Wi-Fi networks. When your time with the side hustle comes to an end, the company will typically email you a prepaid return shipping label so you can send everything back in the original box.

If you are ready to stop worrying about whether your home setup is good enough and want to explore a gig that invests in your workspace from day one, take a look at the vetted opportunities listed below to find a role that fits your weekly schedule.
