If you have spent any time researching manifestation techniques online, you have likely run into the 369 method. Popularized by Nikola Tesla’s supposed obsession with the numbers 3, 6, and 9, the practice is straightforward on paper: you write down your specific manifestation statement three times in the morning, six times in the afternoon, and nine times at night. 

For most people sitting at their kitchen table, this is easy to track. But things get complicated the moment you travel. 

As someone who frequently flies between the US East Coast and Western Europe—and occasionally takes work calls from Asia—I quickly realized that blindly following a rigid 369 routine while crossing time zones leads to burnout rather than alignment. When your internal clock is scrambled, forcing yourself to stay awake until midnight in a hotel room just to scratch out nine repetitions of a script defeats the entire purpose of the exercise.

Here is how I adapted the 369 method for erratic schedules and multi-time-zone travel without losing the momentum of my scripting practice.

## Redefining "Morning," "Afternoon," and "Night" on the Road

The biggest mistake I made early on was treating "morning," "afternoon," and "night" as strict clock times tied to my home zone. If my body thinks it is 3:00 AM, but the local time says 9:00 AM, my mental state is not receptive to focused scripting. 

Instead of looking at local time or home time, anchor your three daily sessions to your biological transitions. 

Your morning session happens whenever you wake up and have your first cup of coffee or tea, regardless of whether that is 6:00 AM in New York or noon in London. Your afternoon session happens during your midday break or transition—the midpoint of your active day. Your night session happens during your wind-down window, right before you attempt to sleep in your current location. 

By tying the numbers to daily habits rather than rigid hours on a wristwatch, you keep the ritual grounded in your actual physical reality. 

## Handling Jet Lag and Disrupted Sleep Cycles

When you cross multiple time zones, your sleep schedule inevitably breaks down. On days when I arrive at a destination completely exhausted, trying to write my scripting repetitions nine times can feel like a chore rather than a focused intention. 

When your schedule is in freefall, use these practical adjustments to stay consistent:

*   **Shrink the physical writing:** If you are too exhausted to sit up and write longhand in a journal, use a digital note on your phone for the 9-rep night session, or shorten the phrasing into a punchy mantra you can write quickly.
*   **Merge sessions during massive shifts:** If you are dealing with a 12-hour time difference and your day is completely compressed by travel, it is acceptable to compress the schedule temporarily. Do your morning three and afternoon six back-to-back if you missed the morning window entirely due to a red-eye flight, but always keep the nine-repetition night session sacred right before sleep.
*   **Focus on the feeling over the clock:** The energy behind the 369 method matters far more than the exact math of Greenwich Mean Time. If you miss an afternoon session because you were stuck on a tarmac, do not try to double up retroactively. Simply pick up with the next natural phase of your day.

## Adjusting Your Scripting Language for Global Travel

When you are manifesting while traveling for work or relocation, your scripting statements often change. You might be trying to manifest a smooth transition, a successful international meeting, or a seamless apartment hunt in a new city. 

When updating your scripts for a mobile lifestyle, keep your phrasing anchored to outcomes rather than specific locations, unless the location is the actual goal. For example, instead of writing "I love my new apartment in Chicago" while you are currently staying in a temporary Airbnb in Berlin, write "I am effortlessly settled into my ideal home." This prevents your subconscious from getting tangled up in the physical geography of where your physical body happens to be sleeping that week.

Your scripting should travel with you just as easily as your passport. By treating the 369 method as a flexible framework rather than a rigid cage, you can maintain your manifestation practice no matter how many times you cross a meridian. 

As you start experimenting with adjusting your scripting routine around travel and changing schedules, it helps to have a reliable system to track your progress and keep your intentions clear.
